Thrust Royale – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Thrust Royale (“the game”, “we”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by F AR. You can contact us at thrustroyale@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

No account needed. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, password or contact details. The only time it asks for an email address is if you choose to buy tickets (see “Purchases” below).

Purchases. You can optionally buy tickets with real money. When you do, you enter an email address, which is sent to our payment provider, Paystack, so it can send you a receipt. The payment itself (card, bank transfer or USSD) is completed on Paystack’s page; we never see or store your card or bank details. We store a record of each payment linked to your anonymous online save: the Paystack reference, the pack you bought, the amount and the date, plus the number of tickets you have bought. We use this to deliver your tickets, prevent double credits and handle refunds. Your email address is kept in your own device’s local storage so you do not have to type it again; we do not keep it on our server.

Online save and anti-cheat. To keep the game fair, the game automatically creates an anonymous online save the first time it connects. It has a random ID and no name, email or password. We store:

  • your game progress and settings (for example coins, ranks, unlocks, emotes, player name), so the server can check that progress is valid;
  • technical data needed to protect saves from cheating, such as a fingerprint of the game version, counters that detect impossible progress, and the difference between your device’s clock and our server’s clock. If a save is rejected as invalid, we keep a copy of the rejected save and the previous save in a security log, together with the reason. Anonymous saves with repeated rejected saves may be locked.

Your progress is also stored on your device. The anonymous online save is tied to this installation only; it cannot be used to sign in on another device, and clearing the game’s data may disconnect you from it. You can turn it off and delete it at any time (see section 4).

Rewarded ads. The game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, watching an ad to earn a bonus). Ads are shown only if you choose to watch one. When an ad is shown, our ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect and use information such as your IP address, device and app information, your device’s advertising identifier, and how you interact with the ad, under Google’s own privacy policy. We do not receive this information and do not give the ad provider your account, email or game progress. Where available, you can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Online matches. Friend and online matches connect players directly to each other (peer-to-peer). To make this work, your device’s IP address is shared with the other players in the match and with connection-setup (signalling, STUN and relay) services. Your player name and in-game actions are sent to the other players in the match. We do not record voice or video, and the game does not use your microphone or camera.

Stored on your device. The game uses your browser’s or app’s local storage to keep your progress, settings, control layout, sign-in session and small flags (such as whether you have seen the tutorial). The game also uses vibration for feedback and can copy a room code to your clipboard when you tap the copy button. It does not read your clipboard.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, and the game does not use third-party analytics. We do not ourselves collect your advertising identifier; see “Rewarded ads” above for what our ad provider may collect when you watch an ad.

2. How we use information

  • to run the game, including the anonymous online save and online matches;
  • to keep the game fair by detecting invalid or tampered saves and by using a trusted server clock for daily limits and seasons;
  • to let you watch optional rewarded ads and receive the reward;
  • to process purchases, deliver your tickets and handle refunds and payment disputes;
  • to answer you if you contact us for help.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the information we collect (such as your game progress) for advertising.

3. Services we use

The game relies on these providers, who process data on our behalf or to deliver the service:

  • Supabase – anonymous online save storage, save validation and a trusted server clock.
  • PeerJS (connection broker) and STUN/relay servers run by Google, Cloudflare, Twilio, Metered and PeerJS – to connect players in online matches. The PeerJS code library is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network, which can see your IP address when it is downloaded.
  • Paystack – to process payments for tickets. Paystack collects the details you enter at checkout (such as your email and payment details) under its own privacy policy and tells us whether the payment succeeded.
  • Google AdMob – to show optional rewarded ads, as described in section 1.
  • Google Play – if you install the game through the Play Store, Google handles the download and any purchases under its own privacy policy.

4. Retention and deletion

The anonymous online save is kept while it exists. You can delete it at any time inside the game (Settings → Basic → Online save → Delete online data). This also deletes the related security-log entries, your ticket balance and the purchase records stored on our server (Paystack keeps its own payment records), turns online saving off, and keeps your progress on your device. Because the online save has no email or name, if you ask us by email to delete it we may need your in-game player name and approximate dates to find it. Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the game’s data or uninstalling it.

5. Security

Connections to our save services use HTTPS. Saves are checked by the server so that progress cannot be edited directly from the device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the game shows ads, it is intended for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age of digital consent where you live, if higher). If you believe a child’s data has been stored, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. To use these rights, contact us at the email address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
