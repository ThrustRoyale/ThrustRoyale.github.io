1. What you can buy

Tickets are a virtual currency inside Thrust Royale. You use them to unlock items in the Store, or to swap for coins. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred.

Payments are processed by Paystack (card, bank transfer or USSD). We never see or store your card or bank details.

2. Delivery

Tickets are added to your game account automatically, usually within seconds of a successful payment. If you paid but do not see them, open the game, make sure you are online, wait a minute, then contact us.

3. Refunds

Because tickets are digital and delivered instantly, purchases are generally final once the tickets are delivered and used. We will make it right, by delivering the missing tickets or refunding you, in these cases:

  • you were charged but the tickets did not arrive;
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase;
  • a payment was made without your authorization (please tell us as soon as you notice it);
  • a technical error on our side affected your purchase;
  • you have not used any of the tickets from a purchase and ask within 7 days of paying.

Swapping tickets for coins cannot be undone, and tickets or coins already spent cannot be refunded. Accounts found cheating or abusing the game may lose their tickets without a refund.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email thrustroyale@gmail.com and include:

  1. the email address you used when you paid;
  2. the Paystack receipt or transaction reference (it is in the receipt email Paystack sent you);
  3. the date, the amount, and which pack you bought;
  4. your in-game player name.

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Paystack. How long they take to show in your account depends on your bank and can be several business days.
