How to install

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file. If Android says it can’t install apps from this source, tap Settings and allow installs from your browser (or file manager).
  3. Tap Install, then open Thrust Royale.
  4. No sign-up needed. Just play.

The APK is not from the Play Store, so Android may show a warning. You can turn the “unknown apps” setting back off after installing.

Good to know

  • Online matches need an internet connection. No account is needed, and an email is asked only if you buy tickets. Training works offline.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads. You only see one if you choose to watch it.
  • Best played in landscape on a phone.
